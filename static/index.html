Bitcoin transaction notifications in Telegram and MAX

A free Bitcoin notification bot and blockchain explorer: watch addresses and transactions, look up blocks, fees, the mempool and the market — in chat or in the Mini App.

What it does

  • Watch a Bitcoin address. /watch <address> notifies you of every transaction paying or spending it — in the mempool, then again once confirmed.
  • Watch a transaction. /watch <txid> tells you when it confirms, in which block and how long it took.
  • Amounts and fees. Each notification shows the amount in BTC and USD, the fee, the fee rate and the expected time to confirm.
  • Blockchain explorer. /info looks up any transaction, block or address: balance, received, sent and history.
  • Fees, mempool, market. Live fee estimates, mempool size and flow, mining pools and the BTC price.
  • Free and open source. Runs on its own Bitcoin Core node. MIT licensed, in English, Russian and Spanish.
